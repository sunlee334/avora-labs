We are asked often about when an order leaves us, so here is what we know today.

## What is settled

- We ship within Korea only
- You can track an order with the order number and the phone number used at checkout
- Once a tracking number exists, the carrier's page is one tap away

## What is not settled yet

Shipping fees and delivery times are not confirmed. We will publish them together
with the payment methods. We would rather leave a blank than write a promise we
have not verified.

Exchange and return steps are blank for the same reason.

## Questions

For anything else about shipping, leave a question at our support page.
If you ordered as a guest, use your order number and phone; if you signed in,
use your account page.
